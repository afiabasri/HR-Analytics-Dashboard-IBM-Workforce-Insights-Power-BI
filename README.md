# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard — IBM Workforce Insights

> **Turn workforce data into smarter decisions.**
> Explore attrition trends, salary benchmarks, and performance metrics — all in one place.

---

## 🗂️ Project Overview

This is a **3-page interactive HR Analytics Dashboard** built on the IBM HR Dataset (1,470 employees). The goal is to help HR teams and business leaders identify attrition risks, understand workforce patterns, and take data-driven action before talent walks out the door.

---

## 📌 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.1% (237 employees left) |
| Avg Salary | $6,503 (+3.2% YoY) |
| Avg Performance Score | 3.15 / 4.0 |
| Avg Tenure | 7.01 years |

---

## 🔍 Dashboard Insights

- 🔴 **Sales** department has the **highest attrition rate**
- ⏰ Employees working **overtime are 3× more likely to leave** (30.5% vs 10.4%)
- 👶 **18–25 age group** has the **highest attrition at 31%**
- 💸 **Sales Representatives** are the most underpaid role at **$2,628/mo**
- ⚖️ **424 employees** scored 1–2 on work-life balance — a silent retention risk
- 👨‍💼 **Managers** earn the highest avg salary at **$17.2K/mo**

---

## 💡 Recommendations (Page 3)

| Priority | Recommendation | Action |
|----------|---------------|--------|
| 🔴 High | Control Overtime | Cap at 10 hrs/week, offer compensatory leave |
| 🔴 High | Revise Compensation Bands | Benchmark & raise underpaid roles |
| 🟡 Medium | Early-Career Retention | Mentorship programs, clear growth paths |
| 🟡 Medium | Improve Work-Life Balance | Remote/hybrid options, wellness programs |
